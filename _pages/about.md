I am a Computer Science undergraduate at [LUMS](https://lums.edu.pk), working toward a PhD application in December 2026. Driven by a deep enthusiasm for research, I am broadly curious across many areas of computer science — from systems and distributed computing to machine learning and AI alignment.

My current work sits at the intersection of **LLM-based systems**, **preference optimization**, and **AI-driven automation**. I collaborate with [Dr. Ali Anwar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=o3eOVbgAAAAJ&hl=en) at the University of Minnesota, and with advisors at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, Virginia Tech, and Google:

- **[Sem-DPO: Mitigating Semantic Inconsistency in Preference Optimization](/projects/sem-dpo/)** *([Findings of ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1184/))* — a weighted Direct Preference Optimization approach that reduces semantic drift during automated prompt optimization. It improved CLIP similarity by 8–12% and human-preference scores by 5–9% over standard DPO, and was preferred in 82.2% of pairwise human evaluations. Advisors: Dr. Ali Anwar and Dr. Ahmad Faraz Khan (Google).

- **[LADs: Leveraging LLMs for AI-Driven DevOps](/projects/lads/)** *(First Author, MLSys '27 submission, [PDF](/assets/pdf/LADS.pdf))* — a six-agent framework for configuration, deployment, monitoring, and automated repair. It reached 68% deployment success across 180 Dask, Redis, and Ray tasks and repaired 94% of runtime failures within three iterations. Advisors: Dr. Ali Anwar and Dr. Ali R. Butt (Virginia Tech).

- **[SkillGraph: Reusable Reasoning Skills from LLM Agent Traces](/projects/skillgraph/)** *(First Author, MLSys '27 submission)* — a GraphRAG-based skill graph for extracting and reusing diagnostic reasoning from agent trajectories, with a process-level critic that evaluates reasoning quality rather than final outcomes alone. Advisors: Dr. Tianyin Xu (UIUC) and Dr. Ali Anwar.

- **[Agent Memory: Storage and Retrieval of LLM Reasoning Traces at Scale](/projects/agent-memory/)** *(MLSys '27 submission)* — an analysis of 535 queries across seven models, with reasoning traces ranging from under 1 KB to over 71 KB per query, toward co-location and indexing for highly variable semantic workloads. Advisor: Dr. Ali Anwar.

- **[Federated Skill Sharing for LLM Agents](/projects/federated-skill-sharing/)** *(MLSys '27 submission)* — a federated framework in which clients share reusable skill templates and generalized reasoning patterns through a global indexed library while keeping private context and final adaptation local. Advisors: Dr. Ali R. Butt (Virginia Tech) and Dr. Ahmad Faraz Khan (Google).

Outside of research, I was a summer intern at **Systems Limited**, where I built an ERP workflow for procurement, project tracking, and reporting. Selected projects include the [LLM Affordability Index](/projects/llai/) and the [CAPS Counselling Appointment System](/projects/caps/). I also review papers for **IEEE and ACM** conferences.
