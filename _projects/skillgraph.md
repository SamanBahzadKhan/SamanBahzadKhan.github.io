**SkillGraph: Reusable Reasoning Skills from LLM Agent Traces**

Summer 2026 – Present · First author

Advisors: Dr. Tianyin Xu (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign) and Dr. Ali Anwar (University of Minnesota)

Leading development of a GraphRAG-based skill graph for extracting and reusing diagnostic reasoning from agent trajectories.

- Built a **database of reasoning traces** for skill extraction and retrieval on new system incidents.
- Identified shortcut behavior that corrupts learned skills and designed a **process-level critic** that evaluates reasoning quality rather than final outcomes alone.

**Impact:** Submission at **MLSys 2027**.
