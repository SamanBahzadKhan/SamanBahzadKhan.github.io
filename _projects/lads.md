**LADs: Leveraging LLMs for AI-Driven DevOps**

Fall 2025 – Spring 2026 · First author

Advisors: Dr. Ali Anwar (University of Minnesota) and Dr. Ali R. Butt (Virginia Tech)

Co-developed a six-agent framework for configuration, deployment, monitoring, and automated repair.

- Achieved **68% deployment success** across **180 Dask, Redis, and Ray tasks**.
- Repaired **94% of runtime failures within three iterations**.
- Adapted two existing systems as baselines and substantially redesigned the evaluation and technical presentation to better establish LADs' gains over prior approaches.

**Impact:** Submission at **MLSys 2027**. [Download the paper (PDF)](/assets/pdf/LADS.pdf)
